Delete your Bhabhi Thulla 3D account

Bhabhi Thulla 3D: Card Game, by Sigil Solution

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Bhabhi Thulla 3D.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Tap ACCOUNT, then DELETE ACCOUNT, and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Email samiullah0243@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Bhabhi Thulla account. Include your player name and Friend ID (shown on the Friends screen), or the email you signed in with. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (guest ID, email address and Google sign-in link)
  • Your player name, avatar, level, coins, gems, stars, unlocked items and saved progress
  • Your game statistics and leaderboard entry
  • Your Friend ID, friends list, friend requests and game invites

What is kept

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Server backups that may still contain it are overwritten within 30 days. Data collected by Google AdMob for ads is handled by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
