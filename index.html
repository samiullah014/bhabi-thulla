BHABI THULLA

The classic Thulla (Bhabi) card game in 3D. Follow suit, dodge the thulla, and don't be the last one holding cards.

Game modes

  • Classic – play against smart bots and win coins and stars.
  • Pass & Play – play with friends on one phone.
  • Leaderboard – see the top players.

Play as a guest, or sign in with Google or email to keep your progress safe on any phone.

Coins and gems in the game are virtual items only. They have no real-money value and cannot be cashed out.
