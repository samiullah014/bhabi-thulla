Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what the Bhabi Thulla game ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account ID. If you sign in with email, we store your email address and a protected (hashed) password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google.
  • Game progress. Your player name, avatar choice, level, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and game statistics, so your progress is saved online.
  • Friends rooms. When you play online with friends, your player name, avatar and the cards you play are shared with the other players in that room while you play. Room messages are passed through Supabase Realtime and are not stored.
  • Friends. Each player gets a short Friend ID. When you add a friend, we store that you are friends. Your friends can see your player name, avatar, level, Friend ID and whether you are online now. When you invite a friend to a game, the invite (your name and the room code) is kept for a few minutes until it is delivered.
  • Leaderboard. Your player name, avatar, level, stars and wins are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Your email, coins and gems are never shown.

Advertising

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad before some games and a small banner on the home screen. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. In the UK and European Economic Area, the game first asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Nearby play (no internet)

When you use NEARBY, phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot talk to each other directly. Your player name, avatar and the cards you play are sent to the other phones at that table only. Nothing from a Nearby game is sent to our servers.

What we do not collect

  • Your photo. If you take a profile photo with the camera, it is saved only on your phone. It is never uploaded.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location or payment details, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

To run the game: to sign you in, save and restore your progress, show the leaderboard, let you add friends and invite them, and show ads that keep the game free.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Google sign-in and ads are provided by Google. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT. This permanently removes your account, your saved progress and your leaderboard entry. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we add new features, we will update this policy and the date above.
