Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the Bhabi Thulla game ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account ID. If you sign in with email, we store your email address and a protected (hashed) password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google.
  • Game progress. Your player name, avatar choice, level, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and game statistics, so your progress is saved online.
  • Friends rooms. When you play online with friends, your player name, avatar and the cards you play are shared with the other players in that room while you play. Room messages are passed through Supabase Realtime and are not stored.
  • Leaderboard. Your player name, avatar, level, stars and wins are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Your email, coins and gems are never shown.

What we do not collect

  • Your photo. If you take a profile photo with the camera, it is saved only on your phone. It is never uploaded.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location or payment details, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, save and restore your progress, and show the leaderboard.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Google sign-in is provided by Google. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT. This permanently removes your account, your saved progress and your leaderboard entry. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we add new features (for example advertising), we will update this policy and the date above.
